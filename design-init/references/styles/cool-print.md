# Cool print

## Essence

Cool print is the cool, disciplined tradition of great print publications: high-contrast serifs, flat surfaces, hairline rules, and monochrome authority with one sharp accent. It is formal, confident, and typographically driven — nothing decorates, everything communicates. Where soft-print welcomes, cool print commands respect.

## Typography

- Use a high-contrast didone for display type — thin hairlines against strong stems. Bodoni Moda is the reference choice.
- Set body text in a sturdy text serif built for long reading. PT Serif is the reference choice.
- Typography carries the entire design: scale contrast between display and body should be dramatic.
- Labels and kickers are small caps or uppercase with wide letterspacing — the widest of any style.

## Color

- The palette is essentially monochrome: near-black ink on white or paper-white ground.
- Permit exactly one sharp secondary accent for editorial punctuation — kickers, rules, hover states. A signal red is the reference choice.
- The accent is punctuation, not paint: it appears in details, never in surfaces.
- Temperature is cool to neutral; this is the one print style where coolness is correct.

## Shape and depth

- Corners are square. 0px radius everywhere.
- The design is completely flat: no shadows, no elevation, no hover lifts.
- Structure comes from rules — hairline dividers, strong borders around figures, clear column logic.
- Contrast between elements is drawn with lines and whitespace, never with surface color changes.

## Motion and interaction

- Motion is nearly absent: state changes are instant or near-instant color/underline shifts.
- Interactive affordance comes from typographic convention — underlines, accent color on hover — not from movement or depth.

## Second mode (opt-in)

- This seed is light-first; its natural habitat is print-white. Build a dark variant only on explicit repo opt-in.
- If opted in, invert to ink-on-black, keep it strictly monochrome with the same single accent, and preserve the hairline structure.

## Anti-patterns

- No warmth: cream backgrounds, terracotta tones, or soft shadows turn this into a different style.
- No rounded corners, no cards with elevation, no gradients, no texture.
- Do not let the accent spread: red backgrounds or large red areas break the monochrome authority.
- No playfulness in type: a single expressive display face is the limit; novelty fonts destroy the register.
